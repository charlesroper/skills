# Two-stage editorial pipeline with standalone `ste-80` and composable `guardian-style-guide`

The writing framework separates **Stage 1 Drafting** (`on-writing-well`, `ste-80`, `project-narrative` and `cbeams-git-commit-messages`) from **Stage 2 Sub-Editing** (`guardian-style-guide` and `tropes`). `ste-80` is implemented as a standalone skill rather than a fifth Surface inside `on-writing-well` because its core constraints – suppressing personal voice, enforcing hard 20/25-word sentence caps and banning progressive `-ing` forms – directly invert the humanity and variable cadence rules in `on-writing-well`'s Universal Core. Repositories wire these stages together through a thin `## Writing framework` routing block in `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` generated by the user-invoked `setup-writing-framework` skill.
