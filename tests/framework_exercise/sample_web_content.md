# Storage quota options

Upgrade your team storage quota at any time from billing settings to increase asset upload capacity.

## Check team quota limits

When I upload shared project assets, I want to view available storage space, so I can avoid upload failures during team sprints.

Storage limits depend on your active plan:

- **Starter plans:** 50GB storage limit with standard email support.
- **Team plans:** 500GB storage limit with priority support and daily backups.
- **Enterprise plans:** Custom storage allocations with single sign-on integration.
